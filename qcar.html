Research / Event-Based Perception & Ground-Robot Control

Event-Based Lane Detection & Control on QCar2

Connecting event-camera lane perception with vehicle control on a Quanser QCar2, with embedded implementation on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin and integration in ROS 2.

Ongoing research · Not yet published

DemonstrationMy contribution

The research problem

How can an event camera provide useful lane information for autonomous vehicle control? This project explores lane detection directly from visual events, without first reconstructing conventional intensity images, and connects perception with feedback control on a ground robot.

Demonstration

My contribution

  • Developed event-based lane detection using an event camera mounted at the front of the QCar2.
  • Implemented the software on the QCar2's NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin and integrated it with ROS 2 Humble.
  • Worked on PID and MPC vehicle control to connect lane perception with autonomous driving.

Algorithm details and quantitative results will be added as the research is published.
