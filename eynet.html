Research / Deep Learning & Visual Perception

EYNet: Airport Detection in Remote-Sensing Images

Extending YOLOv3 with complementary feature-extraction networks, Shearlet-based visual attention, and targeted training strategies to detect airports across varied scales and complex backgrounds.

Detection examplesMethodPublication & code

The research problem

Airports vary in scale, orientation, and appearance, and their runways can be difficult to distinguish from surrounding roads and other structures. This work studies how a detector can use both learned visual features and directional information to improve airport localization in remote-sensing imagery.

Detection examples

Method

EYNet extends YOLOv3 using MobileNetV2 and ResNet18 as parallel feature-extraction networks. Shearlet filters introduce directional and multiscale information to emphasize runway geometry, while modified detection subnetworks combine the extracted features for localization.

Transfer learning on related remote-sensing imagery supports feature extraction. Data augmentation and hard-negative mining address variation in object scale and difficult background examples.

Research contributions

  • Developed an extended YOLOv3 architecture for airport detection, combining complementary feature-extraction networks and modified detection subnetworks.
  • Used multiscale, multidirectional Shearlet filters as a visual-attention mechanism to emphasize airport geometry.
  • Developed augmentation and hard-negative mining strategies to improve training on challenging scenes.
  • Evaluated detection performance on the DIOR dataset against YOLO variants and other object-detection baselines.
